# Content inventory — representative sample

The real library is personal and is not published. This sample keeps what
the design actually depends on: the category shape, the spread of phrase
lengths, and the tone.

**76 phrases across 11 categories** in the real library. The counts below
are real; the phrases are a representative subset.

What the layout has to survive:

- **Shortest phrase: 3 characters** — "No."
- **Longest phrase: 101 characters** — "Aphasia means my brain has trouble
  finding words. I understand you, I just can't always speak easily."

Both sit in the same grid.

---

---

On tone: the real library also carries blunt and profane phrases its owner
added deliberately. The app is a person's own voice, not a care product's,
and the design is meant to carry dignity rather than softness.
